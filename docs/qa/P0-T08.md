## Advisory audit

**Scope** — P0-T08's shared schemas, registry, metadata, fixtures, documentation,
generator/tests and KB/backlog amendments against audit categories A–F. No audit
of unrelated untracked Stripe evidence or future feature implementations.

Review caught a response-schema consistency gap: a full-client-refund resolution
could otherwise carry an `available` earning. The implementation now rejects
that combination and requires `reversed`; a barber-paid resolution requires
`available`. The regression is exercised in `refund outcomes and monetary
responses reject invalid cents/statuses`. This is contract validation, not the
domain refund calculation or proof of persisted earning movement.

server-owned inputs, scope, dependency changes, no I/O/domain maths in schemas
