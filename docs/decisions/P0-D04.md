# P0-D04 / TRIMR-5 — Recoverable barber reliability

Decision owner: Tony. Andrew confirmed **Option A on 2026-10-02** after the pickup proposal.
The source of truth is `RULE-RELY-06` and the five `CFG-RELIABILITY-*` entries in
`QUICKTRIMR_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`. This record explains the choice and its downstream impact;
it does not implement a reliability engine.

## Decision and rationale

Option A uses a 30-day rolling window and 30 offence-free elapsed days to automatic good
standing. One offence gives a warning, two trigger a 60-minute Available Now cooldown, and
three add search demotion and an admin review flag. Four current offences make suspension
eligible for human review, not automatic. Every subsequent qualifying offence at/above the
limited threshold restarts the cooldown from its own server occurrence time.

Count only the barber cancellations already covered by `RULE-CANCEL-04`, once per booking.
Do not double-penalise missed requests: the existing two-consecutive-miss session auto-disable
continues, without a reliability offence. Client cancellations, declines and early Scheduled
cancellations do not count either. Alleged no-shows/disputed fault need investigation through
existing dispute/admin processes; this decision does not create automatic detection or findings.

The restriction is recoverable without requiring completed jobs. Automatic levels step down as
events age out; a human suspension needs human reinstatement. Corrections and emergency
exceptions are reasoned append-only records, not erasure of history. The barber sees their
standing, effects, cooldown/recovery information and the distinction between review and suspension.

Option B would have retained the same thresholds but used a 60-day window/reset and a 24-hour
cooldown. Andrew chose the shorter, recoverable launch policy. Its trade-off is that raw counts
expose high-volume barbers to review more often; admins should consider volume, circumstances
and the repeated pattern before suspending. The numerical values are the approved business
choice, not a claim that research establishes an optimal threshold.

  describes consequences and evidence-based exceptions for circumstances beyond a host's
  control. Its fees and accommodation-specific rules are not adopted here.

These are marketplace comparisons reviewed during pickup on 2026-10-01, not legal advice or
evidence that the selected thresholds are legally required. No legal-compliance sign-off is claimed.

## Exact interpretation and safeguards

- Days mean elapsed 24-hour periods. Events count in `now - window < occurredAt <= now`.
  The eight-row UTC example in the KB returns to good standing on 2026-11-09 at 10:00 UTC,
  assuming no additional offences or admin override.
- Cooldowns end exactly at their deadline. Aging down does not extend or erase a previously
  triggered deadline, and retries/login/recovery do not restart it. The threshold is evaluated
  for the new offence, not by assigning a fresh cooldown on every read of `limited`.
- `restricted` is demotion after otherwise eligible non-restricted barbers, in both booking
  types. Database ordering must apply before pagination; no per-page reshuffle or invented
  distance penalty. No additional public offence/history field is added to discovery.
- Cooldown blocks Available Now only; suspension blocks new business in both types. The server
  enforces this at activation, search, request creation and acceptance. Existing session enums
  and missed-request reasons are unchanged. An otherwise permitted stop or decline remains possible.
- Existing bookings are not automatically cancelled and earnings are not confiscated.
  `RULE-CANCEL-07`, payment, dispute, earning and payout safeguards remain unchanged.
- Four offences are not permission for a background job to suspend. Human approval, a reason
  and an audit record are required. Aging cannot silently undo a human suspension.
- This approval does not invent a safety-ban/no-show policy, new public reliability badge,
  monetary fine, service-volume quota or notification transport. The existing admin notification
  requirement remains; always-accessible in-app standing is explicitly assigned below.
