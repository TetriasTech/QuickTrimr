## Confirmed decision

- 20% of booked service price only, snapshotted at request time.
- QuickTrimr absorbs payment-processing fees, including fees retained after refunds. No client
  card surcharge and no deduction of processing fees from barber net.
- Proportional commission reversal on service refunds. Round QuickTrimr commission down to whole
  cents; the barber receives the residual service cents. Prorate the original commission using
  cumulative refunded service cents so splitting a refund does not accumulate rounding differences.
- Internal dispute resolved barber-paid retains the normal split.

Option B was a 15% service-price commission with the same platform fee absorption. Andrew chose
Option A. At a 4500-cent service price, the five-point difference is 225 cents of commission;
this is a commercial choice, not a technical default.

QuickTrimr must fund retained processing fees even when its commission is fully reversed. Its
post-processing position can therefore be negative. Neither commission nor this limited cash
allocation is a claim of profit or of money paid into a barber's bank account.

## Boundaries

The illustrative 50% service refund is not a cancellation policy. `P0-D03` still owns the late
client refund, inconvenience payment and its funding/commission treatment. The retained
processing-fee payer is already decided and must not be re-invented there.

This decision does not set GST accounting, Connect/payout fee allocation, bank chargeback fee
liability or Stripe charge architecture. The 107-cent example fee is hypothetical, not a rate,
config value, or guaranteed fee; reconciliation must use actual Stripe-reported processing fees.

## Research informing the choice

Reviewed on 2026-09-30 before Andrew's approval. These sources inform the choice; the approved
business policy is the KB rule, not whatever a vendor pricing page says later.

- Stripe describes retained original processing fees on refunds in its
  [refund documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) and
  [Australian pricing](https://stripe.com/au/pricing). Account terms and pricing can change, so the
  examples deliberately do not encode a live processing-rate formula.
- [Stripe Connect pricing](https://stripe.com/au/connect/pricing) describes separate Connect
  costs. Those are excluded from this decision's example cash allocations; no allocation of
  those costs was approved here.
- The [RBA's March 2026 review FAQs](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/review-of-retail-payments-regulation/2026-03/conclusions-paper/faqs/)
  informed the no-card-surcharge recommendation. The app policy is no surcharge; this record is
  not a legal or tax opinion.
