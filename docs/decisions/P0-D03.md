## Approved choice

- Client cancels Scheduled at or within the existing 12-hour window: 75% client refund, with
  the withheld remainder going to the barber (nominally 25%). More than 12 hours remains free.
- Client cancels accepted Available Now: 50% refund, with the withheld remainder going to the
  barber. Before acceptance, release the hold; no capture or captured-money refund occurs.
- Barber cancels an accepted captured booking: full client refund, zero barber entitlement.
  Existing reliability rules still determine whether a reliability event applies; their
  thresholds were not chosen here. Outside-window Scheduled cancellation remains penalty-free.
- Fully reverse cancellation commission. All withheld cents fund the inconvenience payment;
  there is no extra charge, additional service entitlement, or platform top-up. QuickTrimr bears
  retained processing fees, making its post-processing position negative when fees remain.
- Refunds round **up to the client** in whole cents; the barber gets the exact residual cents.
  Do not independently round both percentages. Terms and the Scheduled window are snapshotted
  with the request, as required for money-affecting config, and cannot change retrospectively.
- The adjusted inconvenience earning becomes available after successful cancellation and refund
  processing, with no open dispute. Pending/failed refunds do not release it. Do not fake
  completion. Availability then feeds the normal payout process, not an immediate bank payment.

Option B would have used 50% client / 50% barber for both booking types. Andrew chose the
differentiated option: greater compensation for the immediate commitment and a lighter Scheduled
cancellation cost. The percentages are a business choice, not a rate mandated by an external source.

The seven authoritative integer-cent KB examples cover uncaptured hold release, both client
late-cancellation cases, outside-window cancellation, barber cancellation and uneven prices. The
107-cent retained processing fee is illustrative, never a Stripe-rate config or a promise.

## Research and launch boundary

Sources checked during the decision research on 2026-09-30:

- [Stripe refund documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) distinguishes cancellation of
  uncaptured payments from refunds, and explains that original processing fees are not returned.
  Reconciliation uses the actual Stripe-reported retained fee, not the example fee.
- [ACCC contracts guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/contracts)
  explains consumer guarantees and unfair standard terms. Obtain Australian legal review of the
  rates, proportionality and disclosure before launch. Approval of this product decision is
  **not legal-compliance approval**, and the terms must not override consumer-guarantee remedies.

This ticket does not settle payout cadence, reliability thresholds, tax accounting, Connect or
bank-chargeback fees. It does not implement cancellation, earning release, payouts, dispute
resolution, migrations, or new API payload shapes.
